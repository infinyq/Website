What is a Digital Human Twin?

A digital human twin is a realistic, interactive representation of a real person — their face, their voice, the way they speak, and the memories that made them who they are.

01

Appearance

Facial structure, expressions and characteristic movement.

02

Voice

Accent, tone, rhythm and the small speaking patterns people recognise.

03

Personality

Communication style, humour, warmth — how they would respond.

04

Memory

Personal history, stories and knowledge, shared by the people who knew them.

Our first focus is helping families stay connected with parents, siblings, partners and friends they deeply miss — built with care, consent and honesty at every step.

Privacy & responsible AI

Built on consent.
Designed with care.

Recreating a person is a profound responsibility. These principles shape every decision we make.

01

Authorised data only

Twins are created only from material the uploader has the right to share — with documented consent from the person or, where they’ve passed away, from appropriate next of kin.

02

Always disclosed as AI

A twin is a representation, never presented as the real person. Every interaction is clearly identified as AI-generated.

03

Your data, your control

Designed around UK GDPR: secure storage, no sale of personal data, and the right to export or permanently delete a twin and its source material.

04

No impersonation

Twins can’t be used to deceive, defraud or impersonate. Public figures and non-consenting individuals are out of bounds.

05

Grief-aware design

We’re building with wellbeing in mind — gentle pacing, user controls and signposting to support, rather than maximising screen time.

06

Honest about limits

A twin reflects what it’s been given. We’re open about what the technology can and can’t do today.
